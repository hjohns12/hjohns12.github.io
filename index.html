hope caneel johnson

line cook / artist / educator

bio: hope caneel johnson is following the call of Rasha Abdulhadi to use their bio to uplift resistance efforts against the US-sponsored, Israeli genocide of Palestinians. You can contribute to the movement by donating supplies, direct aid to families, eSims, endorsing PACBI, engaging in BDS, and organizing with local groups involved in Palestine solidarity work. I hope that more people and institutions are moved to full-throated support, with their words, their resources, their (divestment from Israeli) money. You can contact PACBI@wawog.com if you have questions or concerns, including legal ones, about your cultural or academic organization’s commitment. From the river to the sea, Palestine will be free.

Food

Bread Riot at Communiversity Communiversity was a free, experimental, NYC-based community education offering in 2025. I facilitated a day-long workshop for high school students called Bread Riot, exploring food sovereignty through an anti-criminalization lens.


Kitchen Tool Library Kitchen Tool Library is a project I founded in 2023. It is a platform to facilitate loaning and borrowing kitchen tools amongst people living in NYC. Design work by Clare Largomarsino.


Fermentation 101

Workshop for community gardeners I co-taught with the wonderful Justine Garcia! We talked about the science and art of fermenting seasonal vegetables.

Community Cake

Body Process is a Brooklyn-based collective of somatic and energy work practitioners dedicated to creating accessible spaces for healing, connection, and transformation through body-centered practices. In November 2024, Body Process hosted a meal for people to come together and get nourished. I baked a cake for the event, and it turned out nice. Enjoy these photos of my cake.

Grief ferment

I am grieving the ravages of empire and use fermentation to process my grief.
I am interested to learn about other fermentation-related or food-related grief practices. If you would like to discuss, please email me.
